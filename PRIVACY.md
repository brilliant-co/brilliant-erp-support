# Privacy Policy

**Brilliant ERP**
Last updated: September 16, 2026

## Overview

Brilliant ERP is an internal productivity app designed for a small team to manage daily operational tasks. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle your information.

## Information We Collect

We do **not** collect, store, or share any personal information from users.

## Location Data

The app requests location permission solely to display local weather information. Location data is used in real-time only and is **not** stored, logged, or transmitted to any server.

## Third-Party Sharing

We do **not** sell, trade, or share any user data with third parties.

## Data Storage

All operational data (such as attendance records and materials management) is processed and stored on the company's internal ERP server. The app does not store personal data on the device or on any external cloud service.

## Children's Privacy

This app is not intended for use by children under 13 years of age.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
